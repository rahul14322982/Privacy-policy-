App Overview

Welcome to Voice Detector ("we," "our," or "us"). Voice Detector is designed to assist users in analyzing audio files to evaluate whether a spoken voice is likely human or generated by Artificial Intelligence (AI).

We value your privacy and are committed to maintaining the security and confidentiality of your personal information and audio files. This policy provides detailed transparency regarding data collection, storage, permissions, and usage in accordance with Google Play Developer Policies.

2

Information We Collect

To provide user authentication, maintain account security, and perform voice detection analyses, we collect the following categories of information:

Account & Profile Data
  • Full Name: To identify your user account profile.
  • Age: To ensure age-appropriate service delivery.
  • Phone Number: For account verification and security.
  • Email Address: Used as your primary account identifier.
  • Password: Encrypted for authentication access.
Audio Files & Recording Data

Audio recordings selected by you for analysis. Supported audio formats include:

WAV MP3 M4A AAC AMR 3GP
3

Device Permissions & Privacy Guarantees

Voice Detector adheres to the principle of minimal privilege. We only request permissions necessary to execute features you explicitly initiate:

System Audio File Picker Access

Android's system file picker allows you to choose specific audio files for analysis without granting the app broad read/write access to your entire storage drive or private personal files.

Microphone Permission (Optional)

Not required for importing saved recordings. Microphone access is requested only if you choose to record live voice clips inside the app for instant detection.

Notifications Permission

Used to send alerts regarding finished audio analyses or incoming call scam alerts, subject to device settings and Android permissions.

4

Audio Handling, Processing & Disclaimers

Selective Server Transmission: Voice Detector analyzes audio only after you actively select and submit a recording. The selected audio file is transmitted securely over an encrypted connection (HTTPS/TLS) to our configured analysis server.

Analysis Output: The system returns an estimated result (e.g., "Likely human voice", "Possible AI-generated voice") accompanied by an "AI Score" confidence percentage.

History Logging: Recent analysis scores, timestamps, and filenames are saved to your account history so you can review previous results inside the app.

Important Accuracy Disclaimer

Voice detection results are mathematical estimations provided for informational and screening purposes only. Detection scores are not definitive proof of voice cloning or AI synthetic generation.
